 .
 Enables the PWM nodes of the experimental N5 Pro profile

 every boot (/usr/lib/modules-load.d/). Loading writes nothing to the EC;
 only a regulator such as n5-fangov writes.
 .
 Write gate: PWM writes only with DMI sys_vendor "Micro Computer (HK) Tech

 force=1 are read-only; read-only still sends EC read commands for the
 sensors.
 .
 Risk: with writes enabled a write goes straight to the fan output.
 0 stops a fan; the driver has no lower limit, no temperature check and no
 watchdog. The N5 Pro profile is experimental upstream and verified on one
 host only (N5 PRO/F8NAA, BIOS 1.05, EC 0.14). Handing a channel back to EC
 automatic restores regulation on the CPU channel; the PCIe channel keeps
 its last value and HDD regulation after the hand-back is not shown. On
 reboot and power-off the driver sets the HDD and PCIe channels it touched
 to full speed and CPU/SSD to EC automatic. For read-only operation put
 "options minisforum_n5_it5571 experimental_write=0" into
 /etc/modprobe.d/minisforum-n5-it5571.conf. Details: README.md.
