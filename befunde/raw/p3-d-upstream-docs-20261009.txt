 104  The PCIe header was physically validated: `pwm4=128` noticeably reduces speed,
 105  `pwm4=0` stops the fan, and `pwm4=255` reliably starts it from rest and runs it
 106  at full speed. All four PWM channels return to EC automatic mode after reboot.
 107  The board firmware does not enable or export a safe PCIe tachometer path, so
 108  the driver does not fabricate an RPM value.

===== docs/research/N5-PWM-RPM-REVERSE-ENGINEERING.zh-CN.md Zeilen 8-20 =====
   8  N5 固件已经实现三路风扇转速读取与换算，并通过 **PMC2 主机接口的 `0xD5` 命令族**提供六条单字节子命令。Linux 不需要直接访问 EC 内部 XDATA，也不需要自己读取不稳定的 TACH 硬件计数器。
   9  
  10  | 通道 | 低字节命令 | 高字节命令 | 固件 RPM 缓存 |
  11  |---|---:|---:|---:|
  12  | TACH1 | `0x18` | `0x19` | `XDATA 0x8880-0x8881` |
  13  | TACH2 | `0x16` | `0x17` | `XDATA 0x8882-0x8883` |
  14  | TACH3 | `0x14` | `0x15` | `XDATA 0x88D0-0x88D1` |
  15  
  16  组合方式：
  17  
  18  ```text
  19  RPM = (high_byte << 8) | low_byte
  20  ```

===== docs/research/N5-PWM-RPM-REVERSE-ENGINEERING.zh-CN.md Zeilen 57-85 =====
  57  ## TACH 硬件与固件换算证据
  58  
  59  IT5571 PWM 模块的 EC XDATA 基址是 `0x1800`：
  60  
  61  | 功能 | 地址 |
  62  |---|---:|
  63  | PWM0-7 duty（DCR0-7） | `0x1802-0x1809` |
  64  | TACH1 LSB/MSB | `0x181E/0x181F` |
  65  | TACH2 LSB/MSB | `0x1820/0x1821` |
  66  | TACH3 LSB/MSB | `0x1845/0x1846` |
  67  
  68  固件读取位置：
  69  
  70  - `0x9EDC`：读取 TACH1
  71  - `0xA1D1`：读取 TACH2
  72  - `0x9D28`：读取 TACH3
  73  
  74  固件在 `0xAC1D` 准备常数 `0x0020E6DA = 2,156,250`，随后调用 `0x53B7` 做除法：
  75  
  76  ```text
  77  RPM = 2,156,250 / tach_count
  78  ```
  79  
  80  这与数据手册公式完全一致：EC 时钟 9.2 MHz，TACH 采样时钟为 `9.2 MHz / 128 = 71,875 Hz`，风扇每转 2 个脉冲：
  81  
  82  ```text
  83  RPM = 60 * 71,875 / (tach_count * 2)
  84      = 2,156,250 / tach_count
  85  ```

===== docs/publication/RESULTS.en.md Zeilen 98-125 =====
  98  ### PCIe RPM result
  99  
 100  The three firmware-exported tachometers did not change between PCIe stopped and
 101  full-speed states:
 102  
 103  ```text
 104  STOP: TACH1≈2005, TACH2=0, TACH3≈1279
 105  FULL: TACH1≈2013, TACH2=0, TACH3≈1278
 106  ```
 107  
 108  Read-only I2EC inspection showed:
 109  
 110  | Register | Value | Meaning |
 111  |---|---:|---|
 112  | `0x1848` | `0x08` | TACH0 and TACH1 select their A inputs |
 113  | `0x184f` | `0x02` | TACH2 selects its A input |
 114  | `0x16f4` | `0x00` | TACH0B and TACH1B are disabled |
 115  | `0x16fe` | `0x00` | TACH2B is disabled |
 116  
 117  The IT5571 has three tachometer counters, each switchable between A and B pins.
 118  The N5 firmware configures and exports only the three A inputs. The B pins are
 119  currently ordinary GPIOs; forcing them into tachometer mode could affect other
 120  board functions and is inappropriate for a public driver.
 121  
 122  Final result: PCIe PWM control is fully supported, but there is no safe,
 123  confirmed PCIe RPM feedback. The public label should be
 124  `PCIe Fan (no RPM feedback)` and `fan4_input` should return zero.
 125
